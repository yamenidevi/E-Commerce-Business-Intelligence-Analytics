# QuickKart Analytics — Business Key Insights

## 1. Sales & Profitability

**Finding:** Sales, Cost, and Profit show a strong positive relationship.

**Insight:** Higher sales values are generally associated with higher costs and profit.

**Business Impact:** Helps monitor revenue generation, cost behaviour, and profitability.

---

## 2. Customer Value

**Finding:** Customer sales are concentrated around the central range, while a smaller group contributes higher sales values.

**Insight:** Customer contribution is not evenly distributed.

**Business Impact:** Supports customer segmentation and customer value analysis.

---

## 3. Discount & Sales

**Finding:** The analysed dataset shows a positive relationship between Discount and Sales.

**Insight:** Higher discount values are associated with higher sales within the analysed data.

**Business Impact:** Provides a data-driven basis for evaluating discount and promotional strategies.

---

## 4. Product & Category Performance

**Finding:** Product and category performance varies based on sales contribution.

**Insight:** Top-performing products and categories contribute significantly to overall sales.

**Business Impact:** Supports product and category performance monitoring.

---

## 5. City Performance

**Finding:** Sales and profit contribution varies across cities.

**Insight:** City-level analysis provides visibility into regional business performance.

**Business Impact:** Supports regional performance monitoring and business planning.

---

## 6. Order Performance

**Finding:** The dataset contains 100K orders, with 8,083 cancelled orders and an 8.08% cancellation rate.

**Insight:** Order cancellation is an important operational KPI to monitor.

**Business Impact:** Helps identify areas for further investigation to improve order completion.

---

## Overall Insight

QuickKart Analytics transforms transactional data into actionable business insights through Python, SQL, and Power BI, supporting sales, profitability, customer, product, and operational performance analysis.
